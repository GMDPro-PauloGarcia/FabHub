# Money-out audit: FabHub (read-only, 2026-11-19)

**F1 HIGH: self-approval bypass, because payables.created_by can be written by the client.** The self-approval check in enforce_payable_approval (20261105042430:66-70) compares against old.created_by. Nothing stamps or locks that column. payableToSb sends it (src/data/mappers.js:25), and payables_upd lets every AP role update any column (20261111100000:53-55). *Scenario:* a Finance approver inserts or PATCHes a bill with created_by='Mark', then approves it. *Fix:* set created_by from user_profiles on INSERT and keep the old value on UPDATE.

**F2 MED: a Manager editing a bill silently un-approves it.** trg_payables_insert_pending (20261105042430:96-113) forces 'Pending' on INSERT and has no "row already exists, return" branch. savePayable upserts for Manager/Procurement (App.jsx:9753). EXCLUDED then carries 'Pending', and for an approver this is treated as a decision: status goes to Pending and approved_by is set to the editor. Any approver's stale plain update does the same. The screen still shows Approved (App.jsx:9726-31). *Scenario:* Paolo edits notes on a bill in Thursday's approved run, and Friday's payment is refused as "not approved". *Fix:* add an early return when the row already exists.

**F3 MED: a double-click records a partial payment twice.** The Record Payment and Pay-several buttons (App.jsx:16301, 16391) have no busy guard (Btn, App.jsx:361), and each call mints new ids. The database doesn't catch it: DUPLICATE_BLOCK only stops cash going over the bill, and the same-amount review requires a *different* bill (`pp.payable_id <> new.payable_id`, 20261102113419). *Fix:* add a busy flag to both buttons, and in the database review a second same-amount, same-reference payment on the same bill.

**F4 MED: a Manager can approve his own refill.** approve_payment_run (20261114080000:266-320) refuses only the run's proposer. Its liquidation branch never compares requested_by or payee with the approver. *Fix:* raise if the approver is the requester or payee.

**F5 MED: the 3-way match is only in the app, and receipt entry isn't separated from ordering or paying.** recordPayments (App.jsx:9888-96) checks delivery and trusts a caller flag, `runCleared` (PaymentRun.jsx:247). No payable_payments trigger checks delivery. purchase_requests_upd also lets Finance, FinanceAssistant and Procurement set qty_delivered and status Delivered directly, and receive_po_line admits Procurement (20261107010000:102). *Scenario:* Mark marks a PO delivered, or posts the payment through REST. *Fix:* add a database gate on payable_payments, and allow delivery-field changes only through receive_po_line.

**F6 HIGH: the docs/terms gate and the PM-certification gate turn off on fields the client can edit.** The docs gate applies only when category is exactly 'Supplier' and created_at ≥ 2026-11-09 (20261104090100:56-59). Both columns are client-sent and unguarded. is_subcon_payable checks only category 'Subcontractor' or accounts 5070/5100/5200 (20261027160000:57-60). *Scenarios:* (a) Finance changes a PO bill's category to 'Other', and it is paid with no DR, invoice, TIN or terms check. (b) A bill is inserted with a back-dated created_at. (c) A bill on a WO number is entered as 'Labor' with another account, which skips certification and the WO cap. *Fix:* stamp created_at on the server. Decide which gate applies by matching po_number against purchase_requests and subcon_work_orders, not by category.

**F7 HIGH: issued PO lines can be re-priced after approval.** po_require_approver's issued-UPDATE branch just returns (20261114030000). No trigger checks qty, estimated_cost, supplier or po_date afterwards, and po_split_total keys on the client-set po_date. *Scenario:* Marian approves a ₱48K PO, then raises it to ₱400K, or shifts po_date to dodge the split rule. *Fix:* re-run the approver check when the total, supplier or date changes, and stamp po_date.

**F8 HIGH: a WO's contract_amount can be raised after both signatures.** No guard exists (grep `old.contract_amount`: none). Finance, FinanceAssistant, Accounting and Procurement can update WOs, and the bill and payment caps read the live contract. *Scenario:* WO ₱1M, 50% certified and fully paid; contract edited to ₱2M; another ₱500K is billed and paid with no variation signature. The Cost Control signature also survives a contract change made before the Finance signature. *Fix:* after cc_approved_by is set, refuse (or reset the signatures on) any contract change by a non-Manager, and write it to audit_log.

**F9 MED: PO and WO self-approval checks use requested_by, which the client can edit.** po_line_approver_name and sign_wo / swo_require_approver compare against requested_by (sent by the app, App.jsx:5429), which is never stamped. *Scenario:* Marian creates a ₱45K PO "requested by April" and approves it, or signs her own WO. Managers can approve POs they requested (the database Manager branch has no check). *Fix:* stamp requested_by on the server and lock it.

**F10 MED: a PO approved by Cost Control never gets its auto-bill.** approvePOs calls syncPoPayable (App.jsx:7489, 9553-99) on the QS device. QS is not in payables_ins/upd, so the insert is dropped as 'auth' and the update is queued forever. The toast still says "supplier bills raised" (7493), and nothing backfills the bill. *Fix:* raise the bill inside approve_po, or drop the auto-bill and the toast.

**F11 MED: TERMS_BLOCK is bypassed through due_date.** `v_due := coalesce(due_date, invoice_date+terms)` (20261104090100:84-85): a stored due_date always wins, and anyone with payables_upd can edit it. One-off 20261118010000 set all 190 open bills (₱2.23M) to 2026-11-20, so bills on 30/60-day terms are now payable this Friday. *Fix:* use greatest(due_date, invoice+terms), and correct PO bills before the run.

**F12 LOW: drift and UX.**

- poSplitTotal falls back to the UTC date, while the database uses the Manila date.
- RLS_ROLE_MAP maps "Cost Control" to Finance (core.js:423): if anyone still has that role, their JWT can approve and pay in the database while the app hides those buttons (App.jsx:9800).
- The app resets approval only when the amount changes; the database also resets it when the supplier changes.
- recordPayments reports a rule refusal as "not confirmed by server" and keeps the payment on screen (App.jsx:9923).
- Liquidations have no duplicate-receipt check.
